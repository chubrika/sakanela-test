Silk Development

Silk Development is one of Georgia’s leading real estate developers and the development arm of Silk Road Group – one of the Caucasus region’s largest private investment groups, spanning hospitality, telecommunications, energy, banking, and media.

Silk Development is one of Georgia’s leading real estate developers and the development arm of Silk Road Group – one of the Caucasus region’s largest private investment groups, spanning hospitality, telecommunications, energy, banking, and media.

With 20 years on the market, the company has completed 10 major projects and attracted over $1.3 billion in investments.

Each project is thoughtfully realised to the highest standards of construction, safety, and environmental responsibility – guided by a clear vision to create world-class architecture that blends seamlessly with its surroundings while elevating the way people live.

History

  1. 1997

    Foundation and Expansion

    Silk Road Group is established, laying the groundwork for a diversified investment platform across the Caucasus region.

  2. 2009

    Hospitality Landmark

    A defining hospitality project rises on the skyline, marking the group’s arrival in premium urban destinations.

  3. 2015

    Urban Presence

    The portfolio expands into mixed-use and residential development, shaping how the city lives, works, and gathers.

  4. 2025

    Capital Milestone

    Silk Road Group issues $400 million in bonds — the largest private corporate issuance in Georgia’s history.

Silk Road Group

Companies owned by Silk Road Group

The Silk Road Group is one of the leading private investment groups in the region, operating across various sectors including transportation, technology, trading, media & IT, energy, real estate, retail, hospitality & entertainment, telecommunications, and banking. In 2025, Silk Road Group issued $400 million in bonds, marking the largest private corporate issuance in Georgia’s history.
